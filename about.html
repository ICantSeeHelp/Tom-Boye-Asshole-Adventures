Having a character to punish Tom Boye for his stupid mspaint hijinks in a super serious way was an idea i had for ages, The previous comic before this one I think I messed up the pacing up so bad because part of me was way too excited to work on this but I'm rather proud of my work on Grace, Her outfit was very Bloodborne inspired and her face was intially based off intrega from Hellsing, But the more I drew her she grew into her own thing, I only drew 3 pieces of concept art for her before going straight into the comic since I was worried about getting it a rut of drawing concepts withouth actually working on the comic. If I could go back and change one thing I'd want to encorparate alot of blue, to invert Tom Boye's colour scheme.

I had alot of fun with how Grace gets presented! When grace is properly introduced with her Hero Shot, the comic page's paneling are alot less scribbly, and the comic gets an actual border, almost as if the page itself has to act in order for Grace. My favourite pages to work on were the ones she actually gets to touch tom, so you can really see how nasty Tom's art really is, with his incorrectly filled in anti-aliased lines.
